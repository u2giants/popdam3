# Peanuts art program and WildBrain two-axis model (§6.20)

**Decided by:** Albert Hazan, 2026-09-07

## Decision

For Peanuts, the property is the art program rather than the retailer initiative. For WildBrain, era and creative group are two independent axes, not mutually exclusive choices. Style-guide scraping collects metadata only, not art binaries.

> "ai or our software doesn't yet download the actual art files in the style guide yet. We're only collecting metadata." — Albert Hazan, 2026-09-07

## Why

Preserves the actual creative taxonomy used for licensing submissions without conflating retailer campaigns with properties.

## Rejected

Modeling retailer initiatives as Peanuts properties or collapsing WildBrain era and creative group into one column.
