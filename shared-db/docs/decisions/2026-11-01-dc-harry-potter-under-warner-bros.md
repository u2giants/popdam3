# DC and Harry Potter are properties under Warner Bros (§6.34)

**Decided by:** Albert Hazan, 2026-11-01

## Decision

DC and Harry Potter are properties belonging to licensor Warner Bros, not separate licensors.

> "DC and Harry Potter were not left out. they used to (Wrongly) be listed as their own licensors. that was wrong, they are properties under Warner Bros, which is how it is now."

## Why

Corrects legacy misclassifications where major Warner Bros franchises were modeled as top-level licensors.

## Rejected

Creating or restoring standalone `DC` or `Harry Potter` rows in `core.licensor`.
