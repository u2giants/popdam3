# Scraped submission Properties are 100% canonical (§6.31)

**Decided by:** Albert Hazan, 2026-10-26

## Decision

Properties scraped directly from licensor submission sites (e.g., Disney OPA, NBCU, WB STARLABS) are 100% canonical for submission-portal property lists.

> "properties scraped from the submissions sites are 100% canonical. that should also have been well-documented."

## Why

Submission portals reject artwork unless the submitted property matches their live portal taxonomy verbatim.

## Rejected

Overriding scraped submission-site property names with hand-edited guesses or contract text.
