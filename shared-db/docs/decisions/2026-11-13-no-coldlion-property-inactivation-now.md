# No ColdLion Property values are inactivated now (§6.39)

**Decided by:** Albert Hazan, 2026-11-13

## Decision

Keep existing ColdLion Property values active for now; Ilona and Laura will review and decide on any inactivations later (~3 months).

> "we're not going to inactivate any properties right now. we'll leave that to Ilona and Laura, and maybe revisit in 3 months. document that."

## Why

Avoids prematurely hiding properties that business staff may still need during transition.

## Rejected

Bulk-inactivating ColdLion properties during schema or MG06 cleanup.
