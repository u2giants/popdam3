# DY and DS are one Disney licensor (§6.11)

**Decided by:** Albert Hazan, 2026-08-07

## Decision

`DY` and `DS` represent one company (Disney), not two distinct licensors.

Recorded in §6.11 (2026-08-07): `DY` and `DS` are two spellings for the single Disney licensor.

## Why

Treating `DY` and `DS` as separate licensors fragments Disney properties, assets, and reporting.

## Rejected

Maintaining `DY` and `DS` as independent licensor entities.
