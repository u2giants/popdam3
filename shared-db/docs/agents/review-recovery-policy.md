# Review service recovery policy

Owner-authorized implementation tracked on [#4297](https://github.com/popcre/shared-db/issues/4297), delivered as one atomic, independently reviewed PR #4294. PR #4294 source is incorporated with its history and original attribution; it is not a separately merged prerequisite. This tooling policy preserves the mandatory independent exact-head approval count and guarded merge.

## Two distinct allowances

For a proven unchanged-content group of one PR and review slot, two conservative review rounds are permitted. A paid start consumes a round unless immutable terminal evidence binds the exact issue, PR, head, sequence, reviewer and recorded slot, proves no verdict or artifact, and records a supported reviewer-side service failure. Unknown, running and unrecorded starts remain counted. Negative verdicts never receive a failure refund. Source-history verification remains mandatory; empty commits, evidence-only commits, upstream refreshes and net reverts and changing the linked work issue do not reset capacity.

Supported service failures are wrapper_terminal_failure, provider_unavailable, insufficient_quota, turn_limit_cancelled and reviewer_cannot_read_repository. Every proven failure is bound to its exact issue, PR, head, sequence, reviewer and slot, then tracked separately from review rounds. After two such failures by one reviewer for this unchanged-content group and slot, new draws exclude that reviewer; healthy independent reviewers remain eligible. This bounds service-failure allowance at two per reviewer for that group and slot, in addition to the two conservative review starts. Task allowlists and the live eligible roster can make the actual total smaller. Provider-unavailable and insufficient-quota events may use the existing monotonic provider pause path; no failure class grants an unlimited retry. Historical failure records remain immutable.

This change does not introduce a budget reset or make an exhausted reviewer eligible merely because time elapsed or a doctor reported PASS. If all eligible reviewers are exhausted, repair/recovery needs a separately supported evidence path; the tool refuses with the actual service exhaustion, without requiring unrelated source edits. Unknown historical failures need their original verifiable evidence; no session may invent it to unstick a review.

## Independent assignments

Either required slot can be assigned first. Every live peer is checked before and under the assignment mutex to prevent duplicate reviewers, and the earliest live peer supplies initial policy for a new slot. A slot's own durable policy governs its retries and replacements exactly. A fresh slot 2-or-later draw may widen the earliest peer's allowlist only with a recorded nonempty owner quote and a strict superset; subsets, disjoint policies, unquoted widening, and restricting an unrestricted peer remain refused. The quote is stored only on an assignment that actually widens policy. If slot 1 is absent at the current head, the newest valid tree-identical or content-equivalent prior slot-1 assignment may carry for independence and policy; a verified latest replacement is selected, and invalid replacement history refuses. A returned or refused slot is never revived from an older assignment. No slot-1 prerequisite blocks a later slot. Fresh peer, policy and carried-history reads are repeated under the mutex. Provider eligibility, author-engine exclusion, immutable assignment records, compare-and-swap lease transitions, distinct reviewers and the final exact-head approval count remain binding.

## Automatic failure evidence

The governed runner records a paid-start marker before invoking a reviewer. When an existing recognized terminal service failure produces no verdict-like line in either output stream, it calls the existing guarded failure-release operation using the exact sequence and slot in that marker and the held reviewer lease. Release rechecks live issue/head, no existing verdict/artifact, and ownership under the mutex. A failed release preserves the original diagnostics and reports the recovery refusal. Unknown failures, pre-start refusals and output containing any verdict-like line do not automatically claim verdict-less service failure.

Release does not launch another paid reviewer. The next supported assignment or replacement chooses an eligible independent reviewer within both allowances. A direct inline replacement that has not first recorded its failure remains conservatively subject to existing capacity checks; automatic guarded release closes this path for recognized runner failures.
